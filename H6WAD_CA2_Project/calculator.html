Smart Home Energy Savings calculator

Enter your details below to estimate how much you could save each year by switching to LED bulbsand smart heating controls.

How the Calculator Works

This calculator estimates two types of annual savings based on SEI and ENERGY STAR figures:

  • LED lighting saving:based on replacing 60W bulbs with 9W LED equivalents and your daily hours of use.
  • Smart heating saving:based on a 10% reduction on your annual heating bill,in line with SEAI guidance.
