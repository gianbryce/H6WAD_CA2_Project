Smart Home Sustainability:Saving Energy and Water in Ireland

This project looks at how Irish households can cut energy and water using smart home technology and simple habit changes.It supports UN SDG 7(Affordable and Clean Energy)and SDG 6(Clean Water and Sanitation).

Try the Energy Calculator

Why it matters

Smart home sustainability means using smart technology and responsible habits to reduce energy and water at home.

Irish households are using more energy, not less.The residential sector's energy consumption rose by 5.3% in 2024,and homes now account for over a fifth of all energy used nationally.
