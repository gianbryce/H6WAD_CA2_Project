Why Water Conservation Matters

Fresh water is limited, and demand continues to grow as populations increase, cities expand, and climate patterns change. Conserving water helps reduce pressure on rivers, lakes, reservoirs, and groundwater supplies. It also lowers the energy needed to treat, pump, and heat water.

By saving water, communities can:

  • Protect natural ecosystems
  • Reduce household and business expenses
  • Improve drought resilience
  • Lower energy use and carbon emissions
  • Support agriculture and food security
  • Preserve water supplies for future generations

Water Saving Methods

Making small changes in daily habits can make a big difference. Here are some practical ways you can conserve water at home:

Indoor Conservation Methods

  • Turning off the tap while brushing your teeth or shaving
  • Taking shorter showers
  • Running the dishwasher only when it is full
