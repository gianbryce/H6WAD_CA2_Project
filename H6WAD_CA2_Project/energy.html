Energy Efficiency at Home

Save electricity,lower your household bills and reduce your environmental impact.

What is Energy Efficiency?

Energy efficiency means using less energy to achieve same results.

For example an LED bulb produces the same amount of light as a traditional bulb while using less electricity.

Simple ways to save energy

  • Replace traditional bulbs with LED bulbs.
  • Switch off lights when leaving a room.
  • Turn appliances off instead of leaving them on standby.
  • Use smart heating controls.
  • Lower the room temperature by 1°C.
  • Wash clothes using full loads.

Smart Energy Saving Measures

LED Lighting

LED bulbs use much less electricity and normally last longer than traditional incandescent bulbs.

Smart Thermostats

Smart thermostats can learn your schedule and automatically adjust the temperature to save energy.

Standby Power

Appliances can continue using electricity while on standby.Switch them off completely when not in use.
