Energy Efficiency at Home

Three upgrades-Lighting,heating controls,and appliance choices-account for most of the energy a household can realistically save without a full renovation.

LED Lighting

Swapping old incandescent bulbs for LEDs uses roughly 75-90% less electricity to produce the same light.Lighting makes up about 11% of a typical home's electicity bill

Smart Thermostats

Smart heating controls typically cut 8-12% off a heating bill.Turning your room temparature down by just 1°C can lower heating costs by up to 10%.SEAI offers a grant of up to €700.

Standby Power

Appliances left on standby can still draw around 20% of their full running power.Unplugged devices you are not using reduces electicity use with no change to your daily routine

Quick Wins You Can Start Today

  • Replace your five most used bulbs with LEDs first,so they save the most
  • Run washing machines and dishwashers at 30 deg;C and only once they are full.
  • Use timers or smart controls so heating only 30 minutes before and after you actually need it.
  • Unplug chargers,consoles and TVs at the wall when they are not in use.
  • Set your hot water cylinder thermostat between 60-65deg;C to avoid overheating water you don't need
